CAR Index

Contribution Above Replacement is a league-relative composite of professional League of Legends player output. It measures performance above the freely available replacement floor at the same role; it is not a wins total and figures are not comparable across leagues.

The interactive index contains player and team tables across every tracked league, with role-specific components and projected CAR alongside the descriptive season record.
