About JTE Sports

JTE is an independent analytics publication for professional League of Legends. It is built around the CAR Index — Contribution Above Replacement — and the stories that conventional coverage misses.

The approach is empirical. Data is used when it is honest, its limitations are stated, and long-form arguments are built to hold up rather than merely agree with prevailing narratives.

Coverage includes player value, team performance, positioning, trajectories, and game analysis. JTE is pseudonymous; contact is available through Twitter / X.
