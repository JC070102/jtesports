Contribution Above Replacement — league-relative player evaluation across professional LoL.

CAR measures wins contributed above a freely replaceable player at the same position. Figures are not cross-league comparable. WAR/Game is the primary ranking metric.

Teams with high CAR but lower win rates indicate strong individual players underconverting — often due to macro systems and team coordination factors this model cannot capture. The gap between CAR rank and win rank is as analytically meaningful as the CAR figure itself.

What CAR measures. CAR quantifies how many wins a player contributes above a freely available replacement-level player at their position and league. It combines role-specific individual statistics — each adjusted for team context to reduce inflation from winning or losing environments — into a single number expressed in wins.

Replacement level. The baseline is the 25th percentile of qualifying players (minimum 10 GP) at each role within the dataset. A player at exactly replacement level scores 0. The replacement baseline is recalculated per dataset, so figures from different leagues and years reflect different competitive environments and should not be directly compared.

Limitations. The model cannot capture strategic coordination, draft execution, communication, or macro decision-making that doesn't manifest in individual statistics. Players in system-oriented roles (e.g. utility junglers, facilitating supports) may read lower than their actual contribution. CAR/Game is the preferred ranking metric for players with different game counts.

About JTE

JTE is an independent esports criticism platform. The focus is on how competitive games are broadcast, produced, played, and discussed — and where the current standard falls short of what it could be.

Coverage centers on League of Legends and Counter-Strike. League gets the deeper treatment — broadcast critique, caster accountability, team gameplay breakdowns, draft and macro analysis. CS is a sustained comparison point: a scene that has built broadcast culture worth examining on its own terms.

Pieces run long when the argument demands it. Data is used when it's honest. Opinions are stated plainly. The goal is to write criticism the scene deserves but rarely produces for itself.
